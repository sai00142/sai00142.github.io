01 // POSITION

THE QUESTION UNDERNEATH THE QUESTIONS

Whose knowledge becomes legible?

I am a doctoral researcher in Comparative and International Development Education at the University of Minnesota. My work asks how education and expertise acquire—or lose—value as people move across borders and through institutions.

I am especially interested in the administrative machinery behind inequality: licensing systems, recognition rules, labor-market classifications, and the quiet decisions that determine who is permitted to use what they know.

“Data is never only data. Every row is a person moving through a system.”
